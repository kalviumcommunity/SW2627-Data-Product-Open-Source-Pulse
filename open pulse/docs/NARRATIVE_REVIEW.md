# Narrative Clarity Review

> **This document is a template with blanks for you to fill in.**
>
> Task 6 asks you to show the narrative to someone outside the team and record
> what they said. That is a real conversation with a real person, so nobody can
> complete it on your behalf. Inventing a reviewer's feedback would be
> fabricating evidence, which is exactly the habit the storytelling module
> exists to train out.
>
> Below is the setup, the questions, and where to record the answers. Delete
> this block once you have filled it in.

## How to run the review

1. Send the reviewer **`docs/CHURN_NARRATIVE.md`** only. No dashboard, no code,
   no verbal preamble. The document has to stand on its own; if you explain it
   first you are testing your explanation, not the writing.
2. Pick someone who does **not** work in data. A flatmate, a friend on a
   different course, a family member. Someone technical will forgive gaps a
   business reader would not.
3. Let them read it once, at their own pace. Do not answer questions during the
   read; note where they pause or re-read.
4. Ask the three questions below afterwards, in this order, and write down what
   they actually said rather than a tidied-up version.

## Question 2: What should we do about it?

**What they said:**

>

**Assessment.** They should be able to name at least one recommendation
unprompted, with roughly the right owner.

- [ ] Named one or more recommendations correctly
- [ ] Knew who would own it
- [ ] Recalled the recommendations only vaguely
